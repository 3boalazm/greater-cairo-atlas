## New statuses (vocabulary)

- `completed_not_operating` - works are finished (and handed over where that applies) but the facility is not yet open or operating for the public.
- `closed_historic` - the facility has stopped operating (production or service ended); it may remain as a historic or legacy site. It is not the same as `unknown` and never counts as operational.
