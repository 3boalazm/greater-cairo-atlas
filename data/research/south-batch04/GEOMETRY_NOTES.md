# South batch 04 — geometry review

The archived 2026-11-16 `out meta geom` response is the user's Overpass fetch; its query is saved alongside the original `out tags geom` query. Local 2026-11-14 extracts were searched too. The report records candidate OSM types, IDs, versions, tags, source files, decisions and rejection reasons. Candidate discovery uses normalized Arabic/English names and aliases, including hamza, ya and taa marbuta normalization and the requested prefix removal. Exact-name matches alone do not prove identity.

Six entities have 41 new ODbL features: 26 Hasaballah El Kafrawy ways, 8 Yasser Rizk ways, 4 Samira Moussa ways, and one polygon each for NMEC, the Japanese Garden, and Ein El Sira lake. Samira's OSM title expands the seed's abbreviated doctor title; only the four ways within or near the specified CAPMAS areas are included, so this is **partial coverage**, not the whole axis. Unnamed links, similarly named streets, businesses, stops, and places of worship are not used. The four axis names requested by the brief are the only road-name rules; no Hadarat axis way was found. OSM aqueduct ways are lines, not a polygon or point, so Magra El Oyoun remains without geometry under the requested landmark geometry policy.

The lake relation `relation/16855202` has a closed outer and a closed inner ring. The importer assembles genuine member rings, assigns holes only to their unique enclosing outer, rejects open or invalid rings, and never closes a gap with invented coordinates. Feature geometry is validated with the existing pipeline's `feature()` and `validate_feature_geometries()`, then appended without replacing old features. Per-way features retain each real OSM type/ID/version even when an OSM way is already used by another entity; entity-page road geometry is merged and simplified from those source ways with no fabricated connectors. A 0.001-degree proximity check checks same-name road components for corridor coherence but does not join them. Only geometries wholly in the targeted South bbox and within 0.01 degrees of the listed CAPMAS 2017 South/adjacent ADM2 areas are admitted.

Fustat Hills (`way/28800605`) is a named, valid park polygon, but the entity is under construction. The existing feature import requires `official_alignment` and linked official evidence; the available official project references do not establish this OSM polygon's alignment. It is retained as a candidate, not imported or falsely certified. Wadi Degla reserve extends outside the targeted bbox. Coptic Cairo does not establish the full religious complex. Kafr El Elow is a neighbourhood, not the whole Tebbin industrial zone. Asmarat matches are stops and a place of worship, not an exact project polygon. Those are all reported, not silently mapped.

Reproduce from the checked-in inputs with `python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt` and `.venv/bin/python scripts/south_batch04_geometry.py`. Re-running is idempotent; new feature IDs use a batch prefix so existing features are never overwritten. The feature count increased from 2,942 to 2,983; seed entities, facts, evidence and statuses were not changed. The selection-fill implementation remains polygon-only. Road fragments are not a statement of full road completeness, and the remaining 25 entities remain searchable with no geometry.
