# West batch 07 adaptation

- The research branch files were copied unchanged; the 27 new WEST entities were mapped by CSV header, checked for collisions, and added together with all North new entities before either fact batch was resolved.
- 138 facts and 68 evidence records were passed through `adaptResearchSeedSources`; `EV-W7-###` references, supplied verification states, conflicting values and `url_check` notes are retained. Existing entities' names, sectors, types and statuses were not changed. No geometry was created.
- Two source attribute keys begin with a digit, which the existing canonical schema and database constraints do not allow. `6october_separator_length` and `6october_separator_width` are stored as `attr_6october_separator_length` and `attr_6october_separator_width`; each fact's notes preserve the exact original as `source_attribute_key=...`. Values, units, dates, verification and evidence are untouched. The raw source remains unchanged.
- Four incoming rows share exact entity/attribute/value with baseline facts. Each differs in verification state and/or date from its baseline counterpart, so the new sourced claim remains separate rather than silently altering the existing fact's state or date. A later North row also overlaps a West Metro L2 station-count value but has a different unit and date. All seven batch overlaps and decisions are documented in `data/research/batch22-overlaps.json`.
- No curated Arabic labels were invented for new fact attributes in this data-only batch; new untranslated keys are tracked in `tests/fixtures/batch22/untranslated-attributes.json`.
