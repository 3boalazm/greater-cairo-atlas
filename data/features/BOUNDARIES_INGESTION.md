# OSM area boundary ingestion

Versioned OSM `boundary=administrative`, supported `place=*`, and named `landuse=residential` polygons are candidate sources. Landuse-backed boundaries carry `source_kind=landuse` and are considered only when their exact normalized name/alias, point proximity, entity-specific bbox, and city/district area guards pass. City candidates must be 1–800 km²; district candidates 0.05–60 km². Entity-specific minimum areas reject small same-name sub-areas where the source extract contains no district/city-scale geometry: Shubra El-Kheima requires at least 10 km² (the only candidate is 1.82 km²), and Agouza requires at least 2 km² (its only candidate is 0.782 km²). Same-name smaller blocks are superseded by clearly larger plausible polygons; unresolved ties remain unmatched. `CITY-CAIRO` is an explicit exception: relation/4103336 is the governorate polygon and is labeled «حدود محافظة القاهرة», not as a city boundary. The boundary store is separate from point/line features and served one entity at a time by `/api/atlas/entities/{code}/boundary`.

The tracked source is `data/raw/cairo-osm-boundaries-2026-11-14.json.gz`. Rerun `scripts/ingest-features.py` with the saved OSM extracts and existing match rules. The script decompresses the file, writes selected polygons to `data/features/boundaries.geojson`, writes the full top-three audit for every unmatched city/district entity to `data/features/phase-07-5-boundary-candidates.ar.md`, and records hashes, inventory, per-object decisions, license, and attribution in the manifest. If the raw source is absent, an existing generated boundary store is preserved.

The audited 07.4 extract contains named residential landuse polygons for El Narges but not the requested Rehab, Madinaty, or Degla landuse sources; these are marked `needs extract` in the PHASE 07.5 candidate report.
